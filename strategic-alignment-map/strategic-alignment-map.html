Field Notes / Strategic Alignment

What the sorting revealed

The tariff rounds through 2025-2026 functioned as a sorting mechanism. Under the visible pressure, countries were being asked non-monetary questions: whether they would remove Chinese communications equipment, participate in export controls, integrate defense industrial base, host US military infrastructure. The answers revealed alignment that formal alliance status had been obscuring. This map organizes what each country actually did when tested, and what they trade with the rest of the world.
